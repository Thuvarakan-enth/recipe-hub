Appetizers

1) Classic Tomato Bruschetta

Ingredients
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Baguette slices, toasted
Steps
  1. Mix tomatoes, basil, garlic, olive oil, salt and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Spoon mixture onto toasted baguette slices just before serving.
  3. Serve immediately so the bread stays crisp.

2) Stuffed Mushrooms (Quick)

Ingredients
  • 12 large button mushrooms
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Salt, pepper, parsley
Steps
  1. Remove stems and mix stem bits with cream cheese, parmesan, garlic, salt and pepper.
  2. Fill mushroom caps and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 12–15 minutes until golden.
  3. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.
